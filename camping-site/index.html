Buyer's guide

Pick the right sleeping bag rating.

Match your bag's temperature rating to the season you camp in — the single most important gear decision you'll make.

50°F+
Summer nights & warm climates. Lightweight quilts and breathable bags.
30–40°F
Spring & fall. 3-season down bags balance warmth and pack size.
20°F
Cold fall & shoulder-season alpine. Insulated mummy bags.
0°F & below
Deep winter & expedition. 800-fill down and full draft collars.
